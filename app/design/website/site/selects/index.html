Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no subscription, no ads, and nothing locked behind a paywall. There's an optional tip jar if you'd like to support it. I built it for my own photo library and decided to share it.

Do I have to delete my big videos?

No. Selects makes a smaller copy of a long clip and leaves your original exactly as it was. You get a real preview first, encoded from the hardest seconds, so you judge the quality yourself. Both sit in your library until you decide, and the space comes back when you remove the original, which is its own confirmed step. Videos stored in iCloud work too: Selects downloads the original first. HDR stays HDR, and slow motion, Cinematic and spatial videos are never compressed.

Do my photos ever leave my iPhone?

Never. Selects reads your library on‑device using the Vision framework already on your iPhone, so your photos are never uploaded. No account, and no copy of your photos in the cloud.

Can it delete a photo I want to keep?

No. Selects only suggests, and it never suggests clearing a photo you have marked as a favorite. Nothing is removed until you confirm, and it goes through your iPhone's own delete, so removed photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days and can be restored any time in that window.

Do I have to finish in one go?

No. Your keep and clear choices are saved as you go, so you can put the phone down mid‑photoshoot and pick up exactly where you were. Nothing is removed until you confirm, so a half‑finished review leaves your library exactly as it was. Selects remembers where you got to.

What if I'd rather choose everything myself?

Use the Swipe tab to go through your photos one at a time, or tap Browse all to see your library as a plain grid, newest first or largest first. Same safe removal at the end. And when Selects does pick, you can tell it how bold to be: Keep more, Balanced, or Clear more.

Can I clean up just one album?

Yes. Albums is one of the ways in: pick any album you have and Selects looks only there. You can also narrow by dates, a single year or a range, or skip the grouping entirely and browse everything in a plain grid. Whatever you pick, the review and the safe removal work the same way.

What if my photos are in iCloud?

That works. If your library is optimized for storage, Selects fetches what it needs to look properly at a photo. Whether it does that over mobile data is your choice, not an assumption, and anything still in iCloud is never deleted by a failed fetch.

Why did a whole burst disappear?

iOS won't let an app delete single frames inside a burst stack, so keeping one works differently: Selects saves your chosen frame as a new photo, then removes the stack. You end up with the frame you wanted and one fewer pile. Because the keeper is a new photo, it leaves any album the burst was in, and removed photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

What's in Tidy up?

Your whole library, sorted into lists. Worth a look: large videos, exact copies, bursts, and compressed videos whose original is still there. Any time: screenshots, screen recordings, all videos, blurry and low‑light photos, and utility photos like receipts and signs. Open any list, pick what goes, and the removal works the same safe way. A blurry photo is only ever listed, never marked for you: every one starts kept, because a soft photo can still be the only one of that moment.

Which iPhones does it support?

Any iPhone running iOS 26 or later.

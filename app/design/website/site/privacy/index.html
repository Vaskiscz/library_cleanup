Privacy notice

Last updated November 6, 2026

This notice explains what happens to your data when you visit vaclavtrnka.cz and its app pages. The short version: anonymous visit statistics, no cookies, and nothing stored in your browser.

Who runs this site

Václav Trnka, IČO 03940110, Tylova Čtvrť 378, 384 02 Lhenice, Czechia. I'm the controller of the data described here. Write to info@vaclavtrnka.cz about anything on this page.

Visit statistics, without cookies

To see which pages people read and which buttons they use, the site counts visits with PostHog, in PostHog’s EU Cloud. It records the page you open, the page that sent you here, your browser, device type, screen size, language and time zone, how far down the page you scroll and how long you stay, and the named links and buttons you click, such as the App Store button.

It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, which is why there’s no cookie banner. To tell one visit from another without a cookie, PostHog turns your IP address and browser details into a code that changes every day, then discards the IP address. The IP address is not stored, and neither is your location: not even the country. There’s no session recording, no advertising and no profile of you, and nothing ties a visit to who you are.

PostHog processes this on my behalf. The basis is my legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used, so I can make it better (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR). The statistics are kept for up to a year. You can object at any time by writing to me, and blocking scripts in your browser stops them too.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. To deliver each page and keep the service secure, Netlify processes the technical details of your request, such as your IP address, the page you asked for and your browser. It does so on my behalf, and the basis is my legitimate interest in running a working, secure website (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR). Netlify is a US company, so any transfer outside the EU is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Two links take you to someone else's service: downloading Library Cleanup fetches the file from GitHub, and the App Store buttons open Apple's App Store. Their own privacy policies apply there.

If you email me

When you write to info@vaclavtrnka.cz or to an app's support address, I use your address and message only to reply. They stay in my mailbox as long as the conversation needs them. The basis is my legitimate interest in answering you, or the steps you asked for before an agreement.

The apps are separate

Everything on this page applies only to browsing this website. The Library Cleanup app itself contains no tracking of any kind: it runs entirely on your Mac and never uploads your library or any usage data. Its only network call is an anonymous check for a newer version on GitHub. The Selects iPhone app has its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your personal data, for it to be corrected or deleted, for its processing to be restricted, or object to the processing. Write to info@vaclavtrnka.cz. You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority, ÚOOÚ (uoou.gov.cz).
